Scientific results and product descriptions answer different questions. The complete [register of nine business cases](/notes/business-cases/) is maintained separately from the paper notes. This review includes cases that change the requirements for Aligned or offer a concrete infrastructure option. The capabilities below are supported by official descriptions, but their quality and economic impact have not been independently verified.

| Case | What is useful for Aligned | Main limitation of the comparison |
|---|---|---|
| **ConstructAI** | Linking a violation to video evidence, assigning corrective action, and subsequently checking photos/video | The closest published issue lifecycle, but the results are author-reported without sufficient control for exposure and case mix. The detailed PDF analysis is in [Chapter 8](/chapters/construction/#chapter-8) |
| **Buildots + Dot** | Separate layers for construction analysis and conversation over already structured progress data | The [product FAQ](https://buildots.com/PRODUCT/) specifies 24–36 hours to process a capture, with expedited processing available separately. A fast [Dot](https://buildots.com/blog/meet-dot-buildots-ai-assistant/) response does not imply equally fast updates to knowledge of the site |
| **OpenSpace** | Linking history to a location, comparing dates, and human verification | The [progress tracking FAQ](https://www.openspace.ai/blog/openspace-progress-tracking-powered-by-disperse-frequently-asked-questions/) describes AI combined with expert review and typically 24–48 hours for progress reports. This is not the latency of every platform feature |
| **Retrocausal** | Learning a specific operation from SOPs and demonstrations, monitoring steps, issuing alerts, and retaining a product's video history | [Assembly Copilot](https://retrocausal.ai/assembly-copilot/) targets repeatable manufacturing processes. Transfer to moving cameras, irregular operations, and history across shifts requires separate evaluation |
| **Verkada** | Separating indexing from search, temporal filters, and archive scaling | The [engineering description](https://www.verkada.com/blog/scaling-natural-language-video-search-inside-verkadas-breakthrough/) uses CLIP embeddings of person and vehicle crops. This is a useful search design, but does not test the completeness of work histories or the detection of small defects |
| **NVIDIA VSS** | Ready-made components for video ingestion, search, event analysis, and alert verification | [Alerts microservice v3.3](https://docs.nvidia.com/vss/system-components/analytics-microservices/alerts-microservice) supports verification, continuous real-time, and on-demand modes. The infrastructure merits a separate prototype, but the documentation does not establish construction accuracy or correct issue closure several shifts later |

**DroneDeploy Progress AI** is retained as an additional capture-to-report comparison: the same [official page](https://dronedeploy.com/product/progress-ai) mentions minutes in its description and a limit of two hours after upload in its FAQ. Without an equivalent workload, this is not a basis for ranking platform speed. **viAct smart helmet** is relevant for combining identity, location, and incident signals, but the [reviewed page](https://www.viact.ai/iot/smart-helmet?trk=products_details_guest_secondary_call_to_action) does not establish continuous egocentric video recording. **Kiber Assistant** provides a simpler control scenario: a person captures the required evidence, dictates an explanation, and receives a report within the existing workflow. The [Kiber description](https://kiber.tech/kiber-assistant/) is not evidence of autonomous defect detection.

These cases suggest concrete comparisons for Aligned. Memory evaluation must measure both response speed and the freshness of available information. Work monitoring must link an observation to a specific product or operation instance. Corrective action requires showing the original violation, subsequent evidence, and the basis for closure. Useful baselines include periodic site walkthroughs and human-directed evidence capture. The additional value of continuous video from every worker must be demonstrated against its cost and incomplete visibility. An unconfirmed competitor capability remains unknown, so this table does not support a claim of market novelty.
