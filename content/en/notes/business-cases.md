# Business Cases

Comparison of business solutions found during Aligned research. Last checked: **2026-11-01**.

Scope: nine entries covering the seven vendor/platform solutions in the source register, OpenSpace, and the published ConstructAI deployment case. NVIDIA VSS is an engineering reference platform, while ConstructAI is an author-reported deployment, not a verified off-the-shelf offering. The robotics reading list is a discovery resource and is excluded. Research-only systems remain in the paper index. This is a research inventory, not an exhaustive market survey.

Capabilities below follow the linked primary sources. Product pages and documentation verify what is described or offered, not independent accuracy, ROI, or suitability for Aligned. A capability not established by these sources remains unverified rather than absent. Business value and Aligned implications are our interpretation. Pricing and procurement readiness were not audited.

| **Buildots + Dot** - construction software | General contractors, owners and project managers need progress visibility, trade coordination and earlier delay detection. | 360-degree site captures and drone imagery compared with BIM and schedules. Organized around construction elements, activities, trades and locations. | Dot answers questions and follow-ups over structured progress data, including completion and start/end dates. | The platform describes deviation and delay-risk detection. Its Autodesk integration supports exporting detected issues with images and structured issue fields. | Vendor product descriptions and integration documentation. No independent benchmark of continuous worker-video recall or defect alerts was checked. The current product FAQ gives 24–36 hours for capture processing, with expedited processing available separately. This is distinct from Dot answering over already processed data. | Reuse the separation between observed project state and conversational access. Continuous worker execution history needs additional validation. [Platform](https://buildots.com/PRODUCT/) · [Dot](https://buildots.com/blog/meet-dot-buildots-ai-assistant/) · [Issue integration](https://apps.autodesk.com/BIM360/en/Detail/HelpDoc?appId=9028981024066652961&appLang=en&os=Web) |
| **OpenSpace** - construction visual documentation and progress tracking | Project teams need a shared visual record, verified work-in-place status and evidence for coordination. | Site imagery, including 360-degree captures. Progress is organized by location, trade and schedule activity. | Historical images and progress records support comparisons between capture dates. Open-ended memory over each worker's complete recording history was not established. | The progress product describes alerts for unfinished work or blockers. The FAQ describes issue tags for reviewing rework and non-conformance between captures. | Official materials explicitly combine AI with construction-expert review. The checked progress-reporting FAQ gives a typical 24-48-hour turnaround after capture, not latency for every platform function. | A concrete reference for human verification and spatially anchored history. Test the value of fresher automatic observations separately. [Progress product](https://www.openspace.ai/products/progress-tracking/) · [Workflow FAQ](https://www.openspace.ai/blog/openspace-progress-tracking-powered-by-disperse-frequently-asked-questions/) |
| **DroneDeploy Progress AI** - construction reality-capture analytics | Superintendents, project managers and owners need work-in-place reports and trade coordination. | 360-degree walks and drone captures. The page describes progress by location and trade, including operation without BIM or a schedule. | Visual reports show percent complete, installed work, sequencing and changes. A general chat over personal worker-camera histories was not established. | The checked Progress AI page focuses on progress and changes. Broader safety functions must not be assumed to belong to this product without separate verification. | The same vendor page describes reports in minutes in its overview but says within two hours of upload in its FAQ, and still offers an early-access request. These statements are not a matched workload or a validated SLA. No matched workload, independent timing study or defect benchmark was checked. | Compare capture-to-report freshness and the minimum project context needed. Do not equate capture cadence with continuous task coverage. [Progress AI](https://dronedeploy.com/product/progress-ai) |
| **Retrocausal Assembly Copilot / Kaizen Copilot** - manufacturing operations software | Assembly operators, industrial engineers and managers need fewer mistakes, cycle traceability and process improvement. | Cameras observe repeated assembly processes. Onboarding specifies the SOP, records cycles, labels an example and trains a model. MES integration can associate records with product IDs. | Video traceability supports replay of steps and comparisons across cycles, operators and shifts. Kaizen offerings address time studies and process analysis. | Assembly Copilot describes live step tracking, instructions, audible/visual alerts and equipment integration. Corrective feedback is part of the operator workflow. | Vendor documentation and case-study claims. A controlled Aligned-like study was not checked. Manufacturing cycles and workstation assumptions differ from mobile, multi-shift construction work. | A strong reference for task onboarding and linking evidence to a specific work item. Construction needs its own persistent task identity and camera-coverage tests. [Assembly Copilot](https://retrocausal.ai/assembly-copilot/) · [Company offerings](https://retrocausal.ai/) |
| **viAct Smart Helmet / viGENT / viHUB** - wearable and site safety platform | EHS teams and supervisors need worker-level safety status, incident response and cross-site visibility. | Helmet GPS and IoT signals capture location, movement, impact and SOS events. The vendor describes correlation with separate camera analytics. | viGENT is described as answering natural-language questions over helmet activity records and generating safety summaries. | Fall, motionless-worker and zone-related alerts feed viHUB. The page describes automatic incident reports and supervisor notifications. | Vendor documentation only. The canonical page timed out, but an official parameterized copy was readable. The checked architecture does not establish continuous egocentric video capture or visual workmanship assessment. | Relevant to identity, location and incident orchestration. Do not infer video-memory capability from the smart-helmet name. [Official helmet page](https://www.viact.ai/iot/smart-helmet?trk=products_details_guest_secondary_call_to_action) |
| **Kiber Assistant / Repogen** - wearable field collaboration and reporting | Field workers and remote experts need hands-free assistance and structured documentation. | User-directed voice, photo and video capture from wearable/mobile workflows, integrated with Microsoft 365. | Repogen transcribes voice notes, combines captured material into reports and uploads to SharePoint/OneDrive. Teams supports remote expert collaboration. | A person can document and discuss a problem. Autonomous defect detection and automatic confirmation of correction were not established in the checked page. | Vendor product documentation. No independent effectiveness study, continuous archive-search benchmark or autonomous issue lifecycle was checked. | A simpler reference for collecting useful evidence and connecting it to existing workflows. Human-directed capture may be a useful comparison condition for continuous recording. [Kiber Assistant](https://kiber.tech/kiber-assistant/) |
| **Verkada natural-language video search** - security-video product | Security teams need to find relevant footage without manually scrubbing archives. | Detected person/vehicle crops become CLIP embeddings, stored by organization and time, with camera/type filters. | Natural-language queries retrieve matching footage. The engineering article separates indexing and search capacity and describes time-range filtering and cached vectors. | The checked article concerns retrieval. Construction procedure checking and repair tracking were not established by that source. | Vendor engineering account, including self-reported scale and search speed. Its indexing details do not independently establish semantic recall or fine-grained defect sensitivity. | Useful systems reference for growing archives, freshness and independently scaling writes and queries. Crop retrieval alone will not encode complete work history. [Engineering article](https://www.verkada.com/blog/scaling-natural-language-video-search-inside-verkadas-breakthrough/) |
| **NVIDIA Video Search and Summarization (VSS)** - reference platform | Developers and integrators need reusable video analytics, retrieval and agent components. | Stored and live video feeds separate CV, embedding and VLM services. Analytics and agent tools consume resulting metadata and source clips. | Documented agents support video search, questions, incident queries and reports with clip/snapshot retrieval. | The Alerts microservice documents three modes: verification of incident candidates, continuous real-time VLM monitoring, and on-demand clip analysis. Verification retrieves a timestamped clip and stores a confirmed/rejected/unverified verdict. Configured prompts and source coverage determine what is checked. | Official documentation, checked at version 3.3.0. Reference architecture and documented features do not establish construction-specific accuracy or an implemented multi-week repair history. | Candidate infrastructure beneath Aligned. Evaluate available components before rebuilding ingestion, retrieval and alert verification. [Overview](https://docs.nvidia.com/vss) · [Alerts](https://docs.nvidia.com/vss/system-components/analytics-microservices/alerts-microservice) |
| **ConstructAI** - published deployment case | Construction and power-grid safety managers need evidence-linked violations, assignment and rectification tracking. | Helmet, fixed-camera and drone observations, with worker/task/time metadata and an edge/cloud pipeline. | Violation evidence and rectification records form an audit archive. Conversational retrieval over arbitrary multi-week worker histories is not evaluated. | The paper describes localized hazards, risk/regulation reports, assigned corrections and confirmation from later photos/video. | Author-reported Chinese deployments, not independent verification. Table 3 reports 2.3% to 75.8% rectification completion, about 340 to 15 minutes rectification delay, and under ten seconds detection-to-alert. Cohorts, exposure windows and controls are insufficiently specified. | Closest documented issue-lifecycle analogy. Separate detection quality, notification delay and actual correction, and validate each locally. [Paper note](/notes/2026-zhang-constructai/) · [Publisher](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/41452) |

The largest unverified combination across these sources is continuous multi-worker video memory plus evidence-backed defect alerts and reliable correction history across shifts. This observation is about the checked sources, not a claim of market novelty or proof that competitors lack the capability.
